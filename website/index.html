Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 February 2026

1. Overview

Practice Online is committed to protecting learner privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to learners and guardians.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, grade, email/phone number, and login details.
  • Usage information: quiz attempts, results, progress, and platform activity.
  • Technical information: device and browser information, basic logs, and cookies.

3. How we use information

  • Provide access to quizzes, results, and learner dashboards.
  • Improve content quality, platform performance, and user experience.
  • Communicate important service updates (e.g., password reset, account notifications).

4. Data protection

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. Access is restricted to authorised personnel and service providers needed to operate the platform.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (for example email delivery or hosting) only to operate the platform.

6. Learner and guardian choices

  • Request correction of inaccurate details.
  • Request account deletion, subject to legal and operational requirements.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications where applicable.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: info@practiceonline.co.za

Cookie Notice

Effective date: 12 February 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites work properly and remember preferences.

2. How we use cookies

  • Essential: login and security features.
  • Performance: helps us understand what works and improve the experience.
  • Preferences: remembers settings like selected grade filters.

3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect login and platform features.
